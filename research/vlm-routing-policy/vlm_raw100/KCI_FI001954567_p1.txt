Biological Characteristics, Serovar and Detection of toxR Gene against Vibrio parahaemolyticus Isolated from Clinical Specimens

Kim, Shin Moo., Song, Nam Kyung., Chung, Jae Ok*., Shin, Sang Hee*., So, Hyang Ah**., Lee, Gyu Sik., Chong, Yun Sop***

Department of Clinical Pathology, Wonkwang Health Science College, Iksan, Korea*

Department of Clinical Pathology, Wonkwang University Hospital, Iksan, Korea**

Department of Biology, Graduate School, Cheonju University, Cheonju, Korea**

Research Institute of Bacterial Resistance Yonsei University College of Medicine, Seoul, Korea***

Vibrio parahaemolyticus is one of the most important food-borne pathogens in Korea, Japan, and other countries with long coastlines. The aim of this study was to determine the biochemical characteristics, serovar, antimicrobial, susceptibility and possession of toxR gene of the 46 V. parahaemolyticus isolates from clinical specimens. Biochemical characteristics of these were typical, but 28% and 4% of isolates showed negative to Simmon citrate and ornithine decarboxylase test, respectively. Six kinds of serovars were detected in the isolates. Serovar O3:K6 were most prevalent i.e., 85%. Other serovar including O3:K9, O3:K55, O3:K56, O3:KUT and R were very rare. Most of the isolates were resistant to ampicillin, carbenicillin and cefazolin, but none were resistant to most of the other antimicrobial agents including tetracycline, which is the drug of choice for the treatment of Vibrio infection. V. parahaemolyticus toxR gene was detected from all of the 46 isolates. It is concluded that the biochemical characteristics of most of the V. parahaemolyticus isolated Wonkwang University Hospital are typical, that prevalent serovar are O3:K6, and that isolates with antimicrobial resistance with the exception ampicillin, carbenicillin and cefazolin are very rare. Also toxR gene detection by the PCR test can be used for rapid identification of the V. parahaemolyticus in the clinical laboratory.

Key Words: Biological characteristics, toxR gene, V. parahaemolyticus, Susceptibility
