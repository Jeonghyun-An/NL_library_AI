We showed that the efforts of reducing both lead time variability and mean lead time are essential for reducing the cost, which stands in contrast to Chopra et al. (2004)'s paradoxical result that decreasing mean lead time can cut inventories, not reducing lead time variability.

We find that developing efficient heuristics are demanding because the optimal replenishment policy is complex to implement. Field study also indicates that multiple components with different lead time variances can be more appropriate. We will deal with this case in future research. One of the restrictions in this paper was the assumption of at most one outstanding order. Since the problem with multiple outstanding orders is more applicable, we leave this issue for the future research topic.
