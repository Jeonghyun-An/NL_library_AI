[그림: Fig. 1. Histopathologic findings of the otosclerotic specimen in case 1 (H & E staining, × 100). Arrow 1 indicates early active otosclerotic foci with basophilic staining. Resorption of bone around blood vessel, enlargement of perivascular space and many osteoblasts around hypervascular area are seen. Arrow 2 indicates late otosclerotic foci with acidophilic staining. The remodeling and laying down of new otosclerotic bone are seen. Two phases of otosclerosis are seen in same specimen.]

[그림: Fig. 2. Magnified histopathologic findings of Fig. 1 (H & E staining, × 400). Perivascular resorption of the collagen fibrils with demineralization, and osteoblastic lay-down of new bone and scattered osteoclastic resorption are seen. Immature bone, rich in ground substance and deficient in collagen, is then laid down with active resorption and remodeling occurring continuously. The lytic process disrupts the interwoven collagen bundles, causing loss of the characteristic striations. Yellow arrows indicate osteoclastic giant cell.]

[그림: Fig. 3. Histopathologic findings of the otosclerotic specimen in case 2 (H & E staining, × 40). Arrow 1 indicates late sclerotic foci with loss of the normal bony striations. Arrow 2 indicates normal cartilage.]

[그림: Fig. 4. Stapedectomy specimen in case 3 (H & E staining, × 10). Arrow 1 & 2 indicates otosclerotic foci in different phases. Arrow 3 indicates normal cartilage.]

[그림: Fig. 5. Stapedectomy specimen in case 3 (H & E staining, × 400). Arrow 1 indicates more matured otosclerotic bone. Arrow 2 indicates non-mineralized new otosclerotic bone. Arrow 3 indicates normal cartilage. Stapediovestibular ligament are seen on the top of photo.]
