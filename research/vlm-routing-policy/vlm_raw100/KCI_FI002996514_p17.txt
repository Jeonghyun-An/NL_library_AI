대중매체에 나타난 배우 헤어스타일 디자인 분석 259

Analysis of Actor’s Hair Style Design in Media
: Focusing on TV dramas from 2020 to 2022

Lee Kyung Eun*· Jung Yun Seok**

Abstract

Hair designers should have the ability to detect and lead constantly changing hairstyle trends. To this end, hairstyle analysis was conducted based on form, texture, and color as a formative element that can work together to create a visually recognizable and aesthetically attractive hairstyle and create a variety of styles and personal expressions. In this study, the criteria were presented through the analysis of the hairstyles of actors in the original TV drama aired from 2020 to 2022. The dramas chosen for analysis are <Itaewon Class>, <The World of the Married>, <The Uncanny Counter>, and <Reborn Rich>. Through this study, we presented what hair elements are needed for a specific image and various hairstyles, and as a hair designer, we expect to be able to identify customers' needs, conduct smooth consultations, and conduct procedures to satisfy customers' needs. In addition, in future studies, it is necessary to analyze in consideration of the diversity of media such as SNS, YouTube, and video platforms.

Keywords: TV Drama, Actor's Image, Hair style, Hair Trend
