BIographies

Gerald Newbould is Hilton Distinguished Professor of Finance at UNLV. He is the author of nine books and over seventy articles. Dr. Newbould has held senior positions in the Carter Administration, the second Reagan Administration and corporations in the UK, USA and Switzerland.

Percy Poon is Assistant Professor of Finance at UNLV. His main research interests are portfolio diversification, stock return behavior, and relationships between stock and options markets. Dr. Poon has published in journals like Journal of Finance, American Association of Individual Investors Journal, Financial Review, and Financial Practice and Education.

Richard Constand is Associate Professor in the Finance Department of the College of Business at the University of Hawaii. Dr. Constand's research interests include small firm financial management topics and corporate finance issues in Japanese firms. Dr. Constand also performs financial consulting and specializes in the valuation of business investments, closely held firms, and business related losses.

Stephen Witt holds the Lewis Chair of Tourism Studies in the European Business Management School at the University of Wales Swansea, UK. He is also Visiting Research Professor at Mid-Sweden University, Östersund, Sweden. He has published several books, including Portfolio Theory and Investment Management, and numerous papers in academic journals.
