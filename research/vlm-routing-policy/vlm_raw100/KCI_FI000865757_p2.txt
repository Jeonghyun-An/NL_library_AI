Obviously OH protons of calix[4]arene 3 help the HSO₄⁻ binding, presumably by the additional hydrogen bond with HSO₄⁻. Hydroxy proton signal was observed at δ 6.98 in the absence of anions. Continuous downfield shift of OH proton resonance at δ 6.98 was observed upon addition of HSO₄⁻ to the CDCl₃ solution of 3.
