120 사회적 시장경제의 핵심내용과 주요테제
- Abstract -
The Core Content and Essential Theses of the Social Market Economy
Fr. Wottawah Herbert
(Benedict Abbey)
Chung, Yong Kyo
(Yeung Nam University / Sociology)

In this paper we tried to explain the meaning of the so-called social market economy, the historical background and the image of man in this system. Also in this paper we examined this system from an ethical standpoint of human economic activity. We were especially focused on market, partnership and state as the essence of the social market economy. Finally, we formulated the contents of social market economy in different theses. Through this trial we contributed to the understanding of 'capitalism with a human face,' in which human dignity is to be respected in all walks of our life.

Key Words : Social Market Economy, Market, Partnership, State
