A Narrative Inquiry into the School Principal’s
Experiences in Solving School Civil Complaints

Chae-Min, Sohng
(Doctoral Student, Soongsil University)

〈Abstract〉

The purpose of this study is to explore in-depth the experience of a principal in wisely solving various civil complaints that may occur in connection with a school and to understand what factors play a decisive role in solving school civil complaints. The specific research objectives is, first, what are the processes and principles that affect the resolution of school complaints? Second, what do school complaints mean to the research participant? The researcher studied his experience as a method of narrative exploration by selecting one person who has more than five years of experience as principal and multi-year experience in the metropolitan and district office of education as a research participant, who is chosen among school principals who have solved various complaints related to school. This study, collecting and analyzing research participants' experiences, found that he was living a life of his own unique educational philosophy that were different from others. Through this study, the researcher was convinced that practicing educational philosophy and values that the school principal or educational administrator should have in the eyes of the civil petitioner could greatly help solve or resolve school civil complaints.

Key words: school complaints, solution experiences, school principals, educational admin istrators, narrative inquiry
