The Cultural Approach of Heo Nanseolheon’s Poetry
The Customs and Love Shown in Chaeryeongok
Kim Eun-kyung*

<Chaeryeongok> is a song for picking lotus rice and is one of the ancient poetry genres, Akbushi. In Nanseolheon’s poem <Chaeryeongok>, a woman appears on a beautiful lake, waits for her lover, meets her lover, confesses her love, and is ashamed of this.

In Nanseolheon’s <Sashisa-Summer> work, “Chaerunggok” is expressed, and it appears as a folk song sung by a female speaker while picking lotus rice in Gangnam with her friends on a summer day.

Nanseolheon also included the contents of “Chaeryeon” and “Chaereung” in three of the five poems of <Gangnamgok>. <Gangnamgok> is a song that recited the sorrows of women and is one of the ancient poetry genres, Akbushi.

Nan Seol-heon alternately arranged poems that recalled pleasant memories and poems that grieved in his lonely situation in the works of <Gangnamgok>. The composition of these poems further maximizes the sadness of women.

In this way, Nanseolheon borrowed <Chaeryunggok> as the form of ancient folk songs and sang about local customs and love.

Keywords Nanseolheon, Chaeryeongok, Chaerunggok, Sashisa, Gangnamgok, Akbushi
