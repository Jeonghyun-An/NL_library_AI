They expand the horizon of life, discovering new value in the Movement. These character creates the Public Self. In The Land, we can find the problem of a Status System and the characters whose selves are injured by this system. To restore the injured self, they have the will of revolution of reality. By the way, in the Period of Japanese Imperialism the characters who have the will ‘bends’ to regaining a Country’s Independence. Being in course of breaking from the consciousness of social position, they creates the Transitional Self.
